## Sources and review

- [Guitar Player, 1998 interview](https://www.guitarplayer.com/players/jimmie-vaughan-reveals-his-guitar-tone-secrets): picking position, pick/finger attack and the Thunderbirds' broad room-recording practice.

- [Los Angeles Times, 1994 profile](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1994-07-27-ca-20400-story.html): Strange Pleasure personnel and production. The roster also discusses the contemporary tour; no track allocation is assumed.

- [NAMM oral history](https://www.namm.org/library/oral-history/jimmie-vaughan): archive description supporting the Freddie King influence.
