## Connections that are worth following

**The same device can do different jobs.** The Tube Screamer now connects Noel's live solo boost with Vaughan's *Texas Flood* tracking setup. That is a concrete shared equipment family with different contexts, not evidence of identical settings, pedal revisions or influence. Existing Stratocaster and Bassman factors also remain reusable rather than being recreated for each artist.

**Performance and construction are distinct dimensions.** The *Texas Flood* participants describe playing together in a circle, often in one or two takes; that reuses the same band-recording factor saved for OCS. Mullen mentions gobos and later vocal work, so this does not mean zero separation or an untouched live final master. [Band interviews](https://www.guitarplayer.com/players/stevie-ray-vaughan-double-trouble-texas-flood).

By contrast, Owen Morris describes *Morning Glory* starting with an acoustic guide and click, then adding the instruments. He also identifies Neve 1081 equalizers driven near distortion and Apogee Soft Limit during mastering. These are final-record sound factors, not guitar pedals. The session-equipment account separately identifies SM57/U87 guitar-amp capture and a Neve VR console. [Morris interview](https://www.oasis-recordinginfo.co.uk/owen-morris-how-i-mastered-morning-glory.html), [engineer/studio-manager correspondence account](https://www.oasis-recordinginfo.co.uk/equipment-used-in-the-morning-glory-sessions.html).

**Effects can cross instrument roles.** The guitar pedal in the account of “The Day We Caught the Train” distorts Simon Fowler's vocal; it is not evidence of Cradock's guitar chain. Its model and operator remain unidentified, with the recording-level claim saved at 84. [Author-hosted liner notes](https://danielrachel.com/writings/ocean-colour-scene-moseley-shoals/).

My recommended comparison is therefore **attack, movement and layering**: do you respond to the pick/string attack and amp breakup, changing texture during a held note, or the interaction of stacked parts? Those are hypotheses to investigate through listening feedback. This research did not measure the audio or establish a causal explanation of Nick's taste.
